Q1. SFML's coordinate system has (0,0) at the top-left with the x-axis increasing to the right and the y-axis increasing downwards. 
Due to the y-axis moving in an opposite direction from the typical cartesian plane where y-axis increases upwards, 
the angle in SFML will rotate in the opposite direction from the cartesian plane.
